# Ad Creative

Match the deliverable: ideas get directions and beats, scripts get actionable shot
plans, reviews get evidence and amendments. Production uses `$generate`; existing
approved scripts need no new strategy exercise. Campaign setup and account work
belong to `$ads`, not this creative method.

## Choose a method

Choose by the product, human situation and available proof; read only the matching
method and its relevant references. Family-specific detail refines this shared
workflow, not a second intake, approval or production handoff.

| Method | Use when | Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UGC | Natural creator recommendation, tutorial, unboxing, review or organic try-on | [UGC](references/ugc/method.md) |
| Commercial | Product-led reveal, controlled demonstration, polished try-on, brand film or visual spectacle | [Commercial](references/commercial/method.md) |
| Lifestyle | Product enables a desirable everyday state | [Lifestyle](references/lifestyle/method.md) |
| Before/after | A matched comparison can show an approved transformation | [Before/after](references/before-after/method.md) |
| Authority | Expert, insider, case or interview explains a supported claim | [Authority](references/authority/method.md) |
| Educational | A viewer question is answered by visible mechanism or explanation | [Educational](references/educational/method.md) |
| Cartoon | Animation or metaphor makes the product argument visible | [Cartoon](references/cartoon/method.md) |
| Street interview | Candid conversation, reaction and a clear product relationship | [Street interview](references/street-interview/method.md) |

Do not impose a presenter on product-only work. A product review needs an opinion
and its basis; unboxing needs a reveal; a tutorial needs understandable use steps.
Choose one primary method unless combining methods solves a specific creative need.

## Shared workflow

1. Read existing product facts, audience, intended human state, available proof,
   claim boundaries, assets, duration and requested output. Ask only for material
   gaps. Never invent testimonials, credentials, numerical outcomes or guarantees.
2. Separate the opening hook from the setting. Connect the opening question or
   action to the product's demonstrated role. A reusable hook without product
   context is not a complete ad. For reference-led work, use `$media-analysis`'s
   creative-breakdown branch only if needed; borrow structure, not creator identity.
3. Apply the chosen method. Preserve its proof logic, visual grammar, continuity
   and non-imitation constraints. For scripts use a useful shot table covering
   timing, visual/action, framing, camera, product interaction, light, sound,
   speech/text and purpose. Keep one dominant action per shot.
4. State Continuity Locks, Proof Status, Claim Boundaries and Generator Risks when
   they affect production. Mark simulated illustrations clearly; they are not
   evidence of real customer results. Exact typography and final overlays require
   a verified finishing path, not an assumed capability of the video model.
5. If creative approval is still required, present one concrete script checkpoint
   before production. Existing approval or explicit delegation within this task
   satisfies it; do not ask again. Planning or review alone does not authorize
   generation or publishing.
6. Hand approved creative directly to `$generate`, including the chosen method,
   shot script, product facts, proof, continuity locks and input assets. Prepare
   stable stills first only when missing identity, packaging, UI or keyframes
   require them; otherwise generate video directly. Do not create another brief.

For static ads, distinguish layout, approved brand identity, product, presenter
and reference inspiration. Read existing brand facts instead of inventing a brand
kit service or requiring unavailable object IDs. Advertising production does not
publish the result.

## Review

Use existing evidence or `$media-analysis` when inspection is necessary. Separate
observed shots, words and product appearance from inference. Audit hook, product
interaction, proof, continuity and close against the selected method. Ordinary
review stops at findings and requested amendments; it does not silently rewrite
or render the ad.
